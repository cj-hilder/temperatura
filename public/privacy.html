Privacy Policy

Temperatura · Effective 17 November 2026

Temperatura is a recipe timer and thermometer app made by Chris Hilder. This policy explains what data the app handles and where that data goes. In short: your data stays on your phone, unless you choose to back it up to your own Google Drive. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and nothing is sold or shared.

Data stored on your device

Everything you create in Temperatura is stored only in your browser's storage on your device. That includes:

  • recipes, the recipe journal and its notes
  • running and completed steps, with their temperature history
  • alarm themes and the sound files you choose for them
  • settings, and the names and identifiers of the Bluetooth thermometers you have added

None of this is sent to us. You can delete all of it at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Bluetooth thermometers

The app connects only to Bluetooth devices you pick yourself. Temperature readings go from the thermometer to the app on your phone, and nowhere else.

Notifications

Alarm notifications are created on your phone by the app itself. No notification server is involved, and no notification content leaves your device.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you connect a Google account, Temperatura backs up your app data to that account's Google Drive.

  • What the app can access. The app requests only the drive.appdata permission. This gives it access to a hidden folder in your Drive that belongs to Temperatura alone. The app cannot see, read or change any of your other Drive files.
  • What is stored there. Backup files containing the app data listed above.
  • Your Google account email address is read so that Settings can show which account is connected. It is kept only on your device.
  • Sign-in tokens. After you connect, Google issues a token that lets the app back up without asking you again. That token is stored on your device. When the app needs fresh access, it sends the token to Temperatura's own small server endpoint, which adds the app's Google credentials and passes the request to Google. That endpoint stores nothing and does not log tokens.
  • Disconnecting. Disconnect in Settings revokes the app's access to your Google account. You can also revoke it at any time from your Google Account's security settings, under third-party connections. Your existing backups stay in your Drive until you delete them.
  • Deleting backups. In Google Drive on the web, open Settings, then Manage apps, find Temperatura, and choose "Delete hidden app data".

Temperatura's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from your Google account is used only to back up and restore your own Temperatura data. It is never used for advertising, never sold, never transferred to anyone else, and never read by a person.

Hosting

The app is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes your IP address and basic request details to deliver the app and protect the service, and may keep short-lived request logs. Temperatura does not use these logs to identify or track you.

Children

Temperatura is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact
